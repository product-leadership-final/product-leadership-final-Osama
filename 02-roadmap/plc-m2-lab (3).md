# Turn Your Strategy into an Outcome Roadmap, Module 2 Lab

## Prioritize your Rocks and your Hard Nos
- **Rock #1:** Validate demand through wholesale customer interviews and pilot commitments, targeting 10,000 units within 90 days and confirming acceptance of the SAR 200 wholesale price before VAT.
- **Rock #2:** Validate the estimated total cost of SAR 120 per unit and confirm a commercially viable wholesale price of SAR 200 excluding VAT, with a target gross margin of at least 40%, including all relevant production and commercial costs.
- **Rock #3:** Validate a safe, reliable and manufacturable 7.5–8.5 kW instant water heater with a compact design, flexible installation options and Saudi Ceramics quality standards, then confirm technical feasibility and readiness for a controlled pilot.
- **❌ Hard No #1:** Do not commit to large-scale production or inventory until wholesale demand, price acceptance and pilot purchase commitments validate the business opportunity.
- **❌ Hard No #2:** Do not add premium features, unnecessary design complexity or costly specifications that increase unit cost without demonstrated customer demand or measurable commercial value.
- **❌ Hard No #3:** Do not reduce essential safety, regulatory compliance or product reliability to achieve the target price, and do not proceed with a business case that fails to meet the agreed cost and margin requirements.

## Show and swap
- **Do the Rock selections feel traceable to a clear strategy, or do they read like a feature list?:** The Rocks align with three clear strategic objectives: customer retention, renewal protection, and improved field productivity. Fixing the Procore integration protects existing customers, the executive reporting dashboard addresses renewal risks, and offline access supports field teams working in areas with poor connectivity.
- **Pick one Hard No and make the case for why it should actually be a Rock.:** The UI refresh could be a Rock if research shows that the outdated interface is reducing customer adoption, hurting sales conversions, or putting renewals at risk. In that case, improving usability could have a measurable business impact rather than being purely cosmetic. Before prioritizing it, we should validate the problem with customer feedback and usage data.

## Review and refine
- **Does every Now item read as a strategic bet, or does it sound like a feature description?:** Yes. Each item focuses on validating customer demand, price acceptance, or product profitability rather than delivering a specific feature.
- **Can you trace every Now item back to one of your OKRs?:** Yes. Demand validation links to KR1, price validation to KR2, and product feasibility to KR3.
- **If someone who had never seen your strategy read the Now column, would they know what problem you are solving this quarter?:** Yes. The goal is to prove that Saudi wholesale customers will buy the product at a competitive price while maintaining sustainable profitability.
