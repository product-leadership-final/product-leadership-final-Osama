# Write a Strategy That Can Survive a Test, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** Wholesale customers can offer a reliable, safe, and competitively priced instant water heater that meets end-user needs and delivers an attractive resale margin.
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** Saudi Arabian wholesale customers targeting price-conscious consumers seeking reliable, safe, compact instant water heaters for residential use.
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** Leverage Saudi Ceramics’ trusted brand, reliable quality, and safety standards to offer a competitively priced instant water heater with flexible installation options and attractive wholesale margins.
- **Capabilities required: what you must be world-class at. What will you build, buy, or partner for?:** Wholesale market validation, cost-efficient product development, manufacturing quality and safety, competitive pricing, and strong partnerships with factories and wholesale distributors.
- **Management systems: the metrics and rituals that keep your choices alive quarter to quarter.:** rack wholesale demand, confirmed orders, unit cost, gross margin, product quality, and customer satisfaction through monthly KPI reviews and quarterly strategy reviews to ensure targets are met and decisions remain aligned with the strategy.

## Name your one hard no
- **Your one hard no:** We will not launch at scale based on verbal interest alone, because validating confirmed wholesale demand and profitability protects our investment and keeps the strategy focused on sustainable growth.

## Write your three OKRs
- **Objective:** Validate strong wholesale market demand and achieve sustainable profitability for the economic instant water heater in Saudi Arabia.
- **KR1:** Secure confirmed wholesale orders for at least 10,000 units within 90 days.
- **KR2:** Achieve a wholesale selling price of SAR 200 per unit or higher, excluding VAT.
- **KR3:** Achieve a gross profit margin of at least 40% per unit while meeting all quality and safety requirements.

## AI pressure-test
- **Which challenge from the AI is most valid, and why?:** The biggest risk is assuming wholesale demand based on verbal interest without confirmed orders. Interest does not guarantee actual sales.
- **What would you change based on the pushback, and what would you defend?:** I would validate demand through confirmed orders and test the target price before scaling production. I would defend our focus on wholesale customers and our commitment to quality, safety, and profitability.
